# What was left out, and why

## Excluded on purpose (as agreed)

- **Last-mile transport responsibility** (SOP §4.5.3, Logistics Cluster role in last mile). No questions.
- **CP-RTS and its link to IMS** (SOP §5.4 and the "for review" box). No questions. IMS stock statuses (Available, Committed, Released, Impaired) are in scope.
- **RMU office and support share (23%)**: not in the game. Its note ("23% of the full budget") and its formula (23% of direct costs, before PSC) do not match.
- **Kitting**: not in the game. The budget tool has no kitting cost line.
- **Solar lamp and bamboo sourcing gaps** (SOP §6.2 highlighted note). Skipped; the SOP says neither has an LTA price, but the budget tool prices both at USD 6.

## Skipped because the source is unclear or still to be confirmed

- Renk warehouse capacity (SOP says "To confirm"; budget tool says 240 sqm).
- IOM South Sudan complaints hotline number (SOP "To be confirmed").
- Write-off authority and value threshold (SOP §4.10 "To be confirmed").
- "PSU" support for CP PDM (SOP §4.8): acronym not defined.
- Annex items marked as still to be produced or updated (SOP §8).
- MHM inside the WASH kit: the SOP lists 1 MHM kit inside the WASH standard kit; the budget tool costs it as a separate fifth kit. The game follows the budget tool's 5 kits and asks no question on where MHM sits.
- Hygiene kit and MHM kit contents: not listed in either source.
- MHM kit endorsing cluster: not stated.
- Which request form WASH partners use (SOP says "SNFI Pipeline Request Form or CPR Form 2026").
- Budget tool figures marked "Assumption" or "To confirm": river and air rates, 4,500 MT throughput and 85% CP share, 60 m³ truck volume, hygiene and MHM kit volumes. The game teaches the logic only and never quotes these numbers.
- Budget tool "Other delivery points" (IOM delivering straight to a location): close to last-mile, so only hub destinations are used.
- Exact procurement thresholds table (SOP §4.1.2): not used, since it is Supply Chain detail more than CP practice and was under review in the SOP comments.

## Numbers used in Budget Logic

Budget Logic uses small, round, imaginary examples (for example "1,000 households, 50% receive a WASH kit"). They are labelled as examples and only show how the tool calculates. The real rates used (USD 200 loading per truck, USD 15 PDM per household, 7% PSC, 1.61% insurance, 0.2% loss and damage, 6 persons per household) come from the budget tool and SOP.
