# What the game leaves out, and the conflicts it settles

Last Mile Heroes teaches the **partner's** side of the Core Pipeline (CP) only. This file
lists what was left out on purpose, and how each conflict between the sources was settled.
The rule throughout: **the CP SOP wins**. Where it is silent, the item is left out rather
than guessed.

**The SOP is still a draft.** The game uses `source/IOM_SSD_Core_Pipeline_SOP.docx`, dated
September 2026 and marked "Draft 1, for management comment". Every item cites an SOP section,
so the game can be re-checked against the endorsed version. `question_map.md` lists every
item with its source.

## 1. Out of scope: IOM internal steps

The player never does these, so the game does not teach them.

| Left out | Why |
|---|---|
| Procurement, LTAs, supplier and budget work | IOM internal |
| Warehouse receipt, stock management, buffers and buffer thresholds | IOM internal. The game shows only what the partner sees at collection. |
| IMS and the stock/withdrawal request (SWR) approval | IOM internal |
| CP-RTS | Out of scope, as agreed |
| `CP_Budget_Tool_v2_FINAL.xlsx` | Budget tool, IOM internal. Not used. |
| Write-off threshold | TBC in the SOP, and IOM internal |
| Solar lamp and bamboo sourcing gaps | IOM internal planning |
| IOM moving stock to response locations (Annex 1 option) | The SOP gives the last mile to the partner only (§4.5.3). See C8. |
| DTM files, Authorization Form NFI Special Template 2026, the agreement Guidance Checklist | IOM internal legal and data files, not partner-facing |

## 3. Conflicts between sources and what the game teaches

| # | Point | What the sources say | What the game teaches |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1, C6 | When a loss is reported | CP SOP and WASH SOP: 24 hours. Annex 4 form: 48 hours. Annex 1: "as soon as possible". | **Within 24 hours of becoming aware** (SOP §4.6.2). The form's 48-hour note is never shown. |
| C2 | Report deadline | SOP: 15-day target, one month at most, both tracks. AG Art. 6 and Annex 1: SNFI 15 days, WASH 30 days. | **Per track: SNFI 15 days, WASH one month** (AG Art. 6; A1), which also sits inside the SOP limits. |
| C3 | Release document | Annex 1, AG, WASH SOP and the Annex 2 field: waybill. SOP Table 11: issuance form for releases to partners. | **Issuance form** (SOP §4.5.2). Waybills are for transfers between IOM locations. |
| C4 | Request route | Annex 1 and the WASH SOP give email addresses; the SOP gives none. | "The cluster pipeline mailbox, CP team in copy", no address. |
| C5 | HRP / HNRP eligibility | Unclear in the WASH SOP | Left out (see section 2) |
| C7 | Which incidents count as loss | Annex 4: theft, looting, security. AG: force majeure. WASH SOP: third parties or fraud. | **Any loss, damage, theft or diversion** (SOP §4.6.2), with a police or authority report where applicable |
| C8 | Who moves stock to the site | Annex 1: IOM may. SOP: the partner. | **The partner only** (SOP §4.5.3) |
| C9 | How long approved stock is held | SOP: 15 days. WASH SOP: 2 weeks from the release order. | **15 days** (SOP §4.5.1, §4.10) |
| C10 | When the report clock starts | Annex 3: from receiving the items. AG, WASH SOP, SOP: end of distribution. | **When the distribution is complete** |
| C11 | Who runs PDM | WASH SOP: the partner. AG: IOM may. SOP: both. | **Two PDMs**: the partner's on the whole process, and the CP team's on a sample (SOP §4.8). WASH partners: within 2 months (WS §5). |
| C12 | SNFI report length | Annex 2 form: "two weeks". Elsewhere: 15 days. | **15 days** |
| C13 | Item codes | Two lists disagree | Not shown (see section 2) |
| C14 | CPR file name and reference | Three file-name formats across the WASH SOP, the form and Annex 3 | The **WASH SOP** format: `SS_WC_CorePipeline_Orgname_Day_Month_Year` |
| C15 | Wau hub for WASH | SOP and the CPR form: yes. WASH SOP: not listed. | **No WASH request goes to Wau.** The Wau hub is hidden on the WASH track, so the game never takes a side. |
| C16 | Distributing through another organisation (R7) | SOP §4.2.1: direct distribution, without an intermediary, is an eligibility condition. AG Art. 9 allows subcontracting with IOM's prior written approval; Annex 2 has a joint-missions section. | **R7 as the SOP states it**: distribute directly. The AG Art. 9 route (subcontracting with IOM's written approval) is not taught: a field partner should never assume it applies. |
| C17 | Where the assessment goes | | **Attached to the request** (SOP §4.3) |
| C18 | PSEA | | **Report immediately through IOM PSEA channels**, bypassing the normal complaints loop (SOP §7.2, §7.3; IN/234). The partner also investigates (AG 8.3), but only after reporting. |
| — | Change of plan after endorsement | SOP §4.4, §4.6.1: written agreement from the cluster beforehand, CP team informed. AG Art. 5: prior written approval from the cluster and IOM. | **The SOP version**: justify in writing, agree with the cluster first, inform the CP team. |

## 4. Choices made for the game, not from a source

| Choice | Why |
|---|---|
| The player's NGO is fictional: Nile Lantern Relief | The player's real organisation appears only on the completion card and in the CSV. |
| All people are fictional (Nyakuoth, Lual, Deng, Chief Mabior, Majok, Ayen) | Content rule: fictional names only |
| Mock forms carry a "Training example" label | Content rule. The real forms are in `source/forms/`. |
| Numbers inside scenarios (households, days, places) are examples | They illustrate a rule; they are not figures from the sources. Rule values (24 hours, 15 days, 3 months, 7 years, 48 hours and so on) all come from the sources and are cited. |
| The completion card says "Training record, not a certification." | The game is not an assessment. |
| Logos | The official IOM, SLSC Cluster and WASH Cluster logos were not supplied and cannot be downloaded here. Labelled placeholders show until they are pasted into `CONTENT.logos` (see README). |
