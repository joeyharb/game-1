# Standard Operating Procedures for the South Sudan WASH Core Pipeline Request Process and Release of Supply (revised Jan 2026)

Exported as text from "SSD_SOP_WASH_CPL_08-Jan 2026-updated_.docx". Wording kept as in the original; contact links left out. Reviewer comments in the original say the CPR form link, contact lists and report template are still to be updated.

## Background

Since 2014, IOM and UNICEF have jointly managed the WASH Cluster Core Pipeline in South Sudan. Due to funding constraints, the pipeline was rationalized into a single management structure under IOM, effective 04 August 2025. Warehouses are reduced to these hubs: Bor (Jonglei), Malakal (Upper Nile), Bentiu (Unity), Juba (Central), Renk (Upper Nile, sub-hub for the Renk return response and Sudan crisis), Abyei Administrative Area (sub-hub for return response, Sudan crisis, outbreak response and access constraints).

The WASH Cluster uses a standardized Core Pipeline Request (CPR) form, to be migrated to an online application system.

## 1. Eligibility criteria

- Initially the Core Pipeline items will be prioritized for WASH Cluster HRP partners. (The original text then reads, as written: "however Based on the 2026 activity-based costing, recommend removing access for HNRP partners". This sentence is unclear in the source.)
- The requesting organization plans to use the items for a WASH humanitarian response and/or preparedness intervention.
- NGOs/INGOs provide a needs assessment report less than 3 months old, a project document as evidence base, a properly filled CPR form, response background and other supporting documents the WASH Cluster may require.
- Partners from other clusters can access the WASH Core Pipeline if they meet the criteria; the WASH Cluster coordination team evaluates relevance with the other cluster's coordination team.

## 2. Request submission process

- Download the latest CPR form for every new request (it is updated continuously).
- Fill all mandatory cells (red asterisk): request date; agency name, type and contacts; project information; brief description of the WASH needs (summary of needs and planned intervention, duration, number of target households, location, planned response date); confirmation of no late (2 months) pending distribution reports from previous requests for the same state; items requested (item serial number, quantity, warehouse code); transport information (link to Logistics Cluster support in the form).
- Check available quantities and choose the warehouse from the online WASH Core Pipeline stock list, which pipeline agencies update on the 15th and 30th of every month.
- All requests (except repeat requests for former PoC sites) are supported by a separate justification document: a thorough assessment or a project agreement.
- Assessments include contextual and social vulnerability, rationale for the supplies requested, logistical access and protection principles, and are not older than 3 months.
- Repeat requests for former PoC sites are supported by the latest DTM or WFP GFD figures or a needs assessment.
- The WASH Cluster may ask for additional documents (project description, distribution plan, previous distribution report, funding sources).
- For preparedness or prepositioning by partners, extra information may be required: warehouse safety and security, storage capacity, insurance and transport plans.
- File name for the CPR (Excel only, no PDF): "SS_WC_CorePipeline_Orgname_Day_Month_Year". The same name is the email subject.
- Send the CPR and justification document by email to the generic address corepipeline@gmail.com.

## 3. Review and endorsement (maximum 5 days with complete documents)

- First check by the national WASH Cluster coordination team (supported by the IOM Core Pipeline team): incomplete requests are sent back; requests from partners with pending distribution reports can be rejected; confirmed requests get a reference "WC-CPR-Month-Number". Maximum 24 hours.
- Endorsement by WASH Cluster Sub-National Coordinators (SNCs): rejected requests go back to the partner; if no feedback after 48 hours, the national level undertakes the review. Maximum 48 hours.
- Approval by the WASH Cluster at national level, then forwarded to the IOM Core Pipeline managers or warehouse managers. Maximum 24 hours.
- The reference number is quoted in all emails, in the same email chain, with corepipeline@gmail.com in copy.

## 4. Supplies release and transportation

- After receiving the approved request, the Core Pipeline Manager and/or Warehouse Manager informs the partner by email of item availability within 72 hours.
- Partners collect supplies from the pipeline warehouse and coordinate and transport them to the intervention locations.
- The Core Pipeline Manager or Warehouse Manager agrees the modality and time of release with the requester.
- Partners inform the collection schedule at least 24 hours before collection; release can be refused without 1 day's notice.
- Partners' staff check the condition of supplies at the warehouse and sign the waybill/release notes; the pipeline agency gives one copy of the waybill and packing list to the partner.
- Partners secure the supplies during transport and distribution until all are distributed.
- Requested items are held for a maximum of 2 weeks from the release order; after that, availability is not guaranteed and release can be refused.

## 5. Training and mandatory reporting

- Distribution to the affected population is the responsibility of the requesting agency.
- Requesting agencies provide training and/or technical assistance on the use of the supplies to beneficiaries, and document it.
- Distribution report on the WASH Cluster template, including training/technical assistance and attendance disaggregated by age, gender and disability, submitted within one month after the distribution is completed. Failure to report can lead to rejection of new CPRs.
- Pipeline managers can conduct spot checks at distribution locations without informing the requesting agency.
- Requesting agencies update the monthly WASH Cluster 5W; failure can lead to non-approval of new CPRs.
- Partners keep all distribution records and share them when required.
- Partners report any loss or damage caused by third parties or fraud in writing to the WASH Cluster and the pipeline agency within 24 hours of the incident, and confirm that items were not damaged at collection. An incident report may be required.
- Partners conduct PDM (standard format) within 2 months after the distribution and submit it to the WASH Cluster and the pipeline agency, quoting the CPR reference. Pipeline agencies may also conduct their own PDM.
