# NFI Distribution Agreement 2026, IOM South Sudan (partner-relevant clauses)

Exported as text from "NFI Distribution Agreement 2026_South Sudan.docx". Wording kept as in the original. Legal boilerplate (warranty definitions, IP, notices, dispute resolution, indemnity, severability) is left out. Article numbers follow the original.

## 1. Introduction and integral documents

1.1 This Agreement defines the roles of each Party in the distribution of the items listed in Article 2 ("Non-Food Items or NFIs"). The activities are to be completed from [start date] by [end date].

1.2 Integral documents:
- Annex 1: Common Pipeline Access Guidelines
- Annex 2: S/NFI Pipeline Distribution Report Template
- Annex 3: WASH Distribution Utilization Report Form
- Annex 4: IOM Pipeline Supply Damage and Loss Report Form
- Annex 5: Analysis Report Template
- Annex 6: IOM Data Protection Principles
- Annex 7: Audio-Visual Consent Form
- Annex 8: IOM Terms and Conditions for European Union-Funded NFI Distribution Agreements

## 2. Non-Food Items for distribution

- For all shelter/NFI and WASH items, the quantity of supplies to be released by IOM will be confirmed by IOM in writing and determined based on the needs assessment report verified by the respective Cluster Coordination Team, Cluster approved duly accomplished common pipeline request forms, and availability of stock.
- Upon signature, the Implementing Partner shall follow the Common Pipeline Access Guidelines (Annex 1) in requesting supplies from IOM.
- Once the Implementing Partner receives the NFIs at the delivery point determined in accordance with Annex 1 (the "Delivery Point"), the Implementing Partner is responsible for all actions related to the transportation, storage and distribution of the NFIs, including the loading of items from the IOM warehouse and transport of the items as stipulated in Annex 1.
- After the Delivery Point, responsibility for the NFIs, including custody and insurance, passes to the Implementing Partner.
- The Implementing Partner shall inform IOM in writing as early as possible if any NFIs are not distributed to the beneficiaries and shall comply with IOM's instructions on how to handle and dispose of the remaining items.
- This Agreement does not entail any financial transfer. Each Party covers its own costs. As per Annex 1, the Implementing Partner covers all costs to distribute the NFIs collected from IOM.

## 3. Beneficiaries and data protection

- Definitions: personal data (any information that could identify a data subject), data processing, data subject (IOM beneficiaries), "need to know" basis (case-by-case granting or denying of authorized access to categories of personal data after careful deliberation).
- The Implementing Partner distributes all NFIs received from IOM to beneficiaries identified through a needs assessment in accordance with the Analysis Report (Annex 5) prepared by the Implementing Partner and approved by the Shelter/NFI Cluster for Shelter/NFI supplies and the WASH Cluster for WASH supplies.
- In no case shall the Implementing Partner add any other names to the beneficiary signatures list, or to any other list provided or approved by IOM in writing, or allow any addition thereto, without IOM's written consent.
- Receipt of NFIs by beneficiaries is documented with: a beneficiary distribution list with thumb prints, biometric registration, signatures and/or other means, kept by the Implementing Partner and available for monitoring and audit; and a narrative distribution/utilization report (Annex 2 for S/NFI, Annex 3 for WASH).
- The Implementing Partner complies with the IOM Data Protection Principles (Annex 6).
- The Implementing Partner preserves the confidentiality of personal data and the anonymity of data subjects.
- Consent: beneficiaries sign the beneficiary signature list before personal data is collected, and the Audio-Visual Consent Form (Annex 7) before photographs or other audiovisual material are taken. The content of consent forms is explained in a language beneficiaries understand.
- Data collected: full name, age, gender, household members (number, age group, gender, vulnerability), place of origin, place of distribution, date of initial displacement, ID number, contact details and signature. The partner also collects photographs on behalf of IOM for verification, reporting and communication products.
- Transfer: shared with IOM when requested, by email in an encrypted, password-protected Excel file; the password is sent by another channel.
- The partner undertakes to: use personal data only for the NFI distribution; transfer data to IOM when requested; apply data security measures; keep strict confidentiality and access controls, with encrypted transmissions; limit access on a strict "need to know" basis; ensure staff, agents and subcontractors respect confidentiality; not process data outside the Agreement; update, correct or delete data on IOM instruction; not disclose data to any third party without IOM's prior written permission; keep data only as long as needed, then transfer it to IOM and delete all copies; notify IOM immediately of data subject requests, third-party (including government) requests, and any data breach.

## 4. Responsibilities of IOM

- Release the NFIs to the Implementing Partner in accordance with Articles 2 and 5.
- Ensure that the release is accompanied by an authorized waybill, signed by IOM authorized staff, specifying items and quantities. On receipt, the Implementing Partner also signs the waybill and submits it to IOM.
- Provide procurement and management of pipeline supplies, regular updates of availability through the online stock update, delivery customs-cleared to IOM key/field hub warehouses, and warehousing services in key hubs.
- IOM may randomly select distributions and conduct post-distribution monitoring (PDM).

## 5. Responsibilities of the Implementing Partner

- With IOM and in coordination with the Clusters, establish a distribution plan (means of distribution, target areas, beneficiary selection criteria) before receiving the NFIs. Provide the Analysis Report and the Distribution/Utilization Report (Annexes 2 and 3) to the IOM Core Pipeline Unit. If security no longer allows distribution in the agreed area, change location only with prior coordination with and prior written approval of the cluster and IOM.
- Comply with the Common Pipeline Access Guidelines (Annex 1, Step 3) on collection and transportation.
- From receipt at the IOM warehouse, the partner is responsible for loading, offloading, transport, safekeeping, distribution and all handling, and for any damage or loss. Reimbursement to IOM for items damaged, stolen or lost through the partner's acts, omissions or negligence is made on IOM's request. For losses caused by force majeure events (Article 10.2), the partner submits the Pipeline Supply Damage and Loss Report Form (Annex 4).
- Ensure the NFIs reach the intended beneficiaries before the completion date, in the condition received. Share the name and contact of the distribution focal person before the distribution date.
- Ensure qualified personnel, security and adequate means.
- Provide all humanitarian assistance free of charge, fees or services. Distribute to all beneficiaries in complete impartiality, regardless of race, religion, nationality, political opinion, gender or disabilities, without linking assistance to any religious, political or other persuasion.
- Report distribution figures and analysis to IOM and the cluster; take part in cluster coordination meetings if needed.
- Provide training and/or technical assistance on the use of the NFIs, documented in the distribution/utilization report.
- If required, take part in an inter-agency (possibly IOM-led) PDM.
- Keep separate records of all NFIs received from IOM and distributed; retain records for 7 years after termination.
- Encourage community groups or committees to take part in beneficiary identification, registration and distribution; hold regular meetings to ratify beneficiary targets and brief stakeholders.
- Assist IOM and donors in site visits at any time.
- Information sharing: non-compliance or sub-standard implementation without acceptable justification may result in denial of future requests or termination.
- Coordinate with the IOM Core Pipeline Unit on distribution monitoring and PDM; provide logistical support to third-party monitors if required.

## 6. Reports

- Distribution report: by detailed location (GPS preferred), by item, with number of beneficiary recipients (families/individuals), no more than 15 calendar days (S-NFI) and 30 calendar days (WASH) after the distribution completion date, using Annex 2 (shelter/NFI) or Annex 3 (WASH). The cluster may withhold release of the next items requested if the previous report is not received.
- Photos of the distribution and the relevant beneficiary database are submitted with each report.

## 7. Use of IOM name, abbreviation and emblem

- Only in connection with the activities under the Agreement and with IOM's prior written approval.

## 8.3 PSEA and sexual harassment (summary of partner obligations)

- Take all appropriate measures to prevent sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA) and sexual harassment (SH) by its employees and associated personnel; follow the standards of conduct of the UN Secretary-General's Bulletin ST/SGB/2003/13.
- Promptly and confidentially report to IOM any allegation or suspicion of SEA or SH; investigate credible allegations, take corrective and disciplinary measures, and inform IOM of the outcome.
- Breach is a material breach: IOM may terminate immediately (8.4). IOM may investigate any allegation itself (8.5).

## 9. Assignment and subcontracting

- The partner shall not assign or subcontract the activities, in whole or in part, unless agreed in writing in advance by IOM.

## 19. Termination (extract)

- 19.1 One month's written notice, or suspension by IOM; immediate termination if the partner breaches the Agreement.
- 19.2 On termination, all items not yet distributed, or distributed in breach of the Agreement, are returned to IOM.

## 23. Special provisions (extract)

- 23.1 The partner displays donor and IOM visibility items at distribution locations; IOM provides them with the released NFIs.
