## Conflicts between sources (flag in Step 1, do not choose silently)

1. Loss and damage deadline: CP SOP and WASH SOP 2026 say 24 hours. The Annex 4 form itself says 48 hours. Teach 24 hours (CP SOP) and do not show the form's 48-hour notice.
2. Distribution report deadline: the agreement and Access Guideline give 15 days for SNFI and 30 days for WASH. The CP SOP gives a 15-day target and a one-month maximum. Per track: SNFI 15 days, WASH one month.
3. Release document: the agreement and Access Guideline say waybill; the CP SOP says issuance form for releases to partners. Follow the CP SOP.
4. Request route for WASH: the WASH SOP 2026 says email to corepipeline@gmail.com; follow the CP SOP if it states a different route.
5. Eligibility: the WASH SOP 2026 has an unclear sentence on HRP/HNRP partners. Leave HRP rules out (agreed).

- SNFI pipeline operation deck, BRIEF Accessing the SNFI Pipeline, How to access the SNFI pipeline (2017), WASH Cluster CP SOP 2022: older versions, replaced by the CP SOP and the WASH SOP 2026.
- Budget, LTA, procurement, warehouse and DTM files: IOM internal, out of scope.
